Frequently asked questions

How does Physical set my goals?

Your weekly aerobic and strength targets come from the 2018 U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines, personalized using the profile you enter (age, biological sex, height, weight, and any factors like pregnancy or a chronic condition). You can change your profile anytime from the Settings screen.

Physical isn't seeing my workouts.

Physical reads and writes workouts through Apple Health. Make sure access is granted: open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Health → Physical, and turn on the categories. You can also tap "Turn On All" when the Health permission screen first appears.

I already paid. How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstall the app or set up a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen (it also appears after your trial ends). Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, and the unlock is included with Family Sharing.

How does the free trial and unlock work?

Physical is free to use for 7 days. After that, a single one-time purchase ($7.99) unlocks the app forever — there's no subscription. The trial is tracked on your device; no account is required.

Can I add my workouts to my calendar?

Yes. Turn on Calendar sync in Settings and Physical will add an event for each workout you log (write-only — it can't read your existing events). You can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Why does Physical ask for my location?

Only for outdoor workouts on Apple Watch (run, walk, bike, hike). With your permission, it records the GPS route so the workout shows a map in Apple Health — using while-in-use access, only during the workout. Your location never leaves your device. Manage it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

How do I use Physical on my Apple Watch?

Install the companion app on your paired Apple Watch. Start a workout on the Watch and Physical streams your live heart rate and intensity zones to your iPhone — including the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity. Add the ring complication to your watch face for at-a-glance progress.

Can I turn the weekly reminder on or off?

Yes. The optional weekly reminder is a local notification you control with a toggle in Settings, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Physical. Physical sends no other notifications.

How do I delete my data?

Your information is stored only on your device. Delete your profile in the app, or delete the app, to remove its local data. Workouts and routes live in Apple Health and Calendar events in your calendar — edit or delete those in Apple Health and Calendar, where you can also revoke Physical's access. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Physical collect or share my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network calls. Nothing is collected or transmitted. Read the full Privacy Policy.
